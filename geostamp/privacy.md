# Privacy Policy — GPS Camera Stamp - No Ads

**Last updated:** 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to **GPS Camera Stamp - No Ads** for Android.

The app is a camera utility for capturing photos and videos with optional GPS, address, date/time, map, QR, and related stamp information.

## Privacy-first design

GPS Camera Stamp - No Ads does not require an account and does not automatically upload your photos or videos to a developer-operated server.

Photos and videos are processed on your device and saved locally when you choose to capture or save them.

The app currently does not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

## Permissions

The app may request the following permissions only when needed for app features:

- **Camera** — to capture photos and videos.
- **Location** — to show coordinates, location accuracy, address information, map content, and location-related stamps. Location is used while relevant app features are active; the app does not require background location.
- **Microphone** — to record audio with videos. You may use video recording without audio where supported.
- **Photos / media / storage access** — where required by Android to save, open, select, or manage media created or chosen by you.
- **Internet access** — for optional online features such as address lookup, map tiles, or weather information.

You can deny optional permissions, although some related features may then be unavailable.

## Location and online services

Some optional features may send location-related information from your device directly to third-party services in order to provide the requested feature.

Examples include:

- address / reverse-geocoding services;
- map tile providers;
- optional weather services;
- map applications opened by you.

Depending on the feature and provider, information such as coordinates, approximate location, IP address, app/version user-agent information, or requested map area may be processed by that third party under its own privacy policy.

Potential providers used by app features may include:

- Google / Android platform location and geocoding services;
- OpenStreetMap;
- OpenTopoMap;
- EOX map services;
- Open-Meteo.

The camera can still be used without many of these optional online features.

## Photos, videos and metadata

Media created using the app may contain visible information that you choose to include in a stamp, such as:

- latitude and longitude;
- address or place information;
- date and time;
- map thumbnail;
- QR code;
- custom text or other selected stamp fields.

If you share such media, anyone receiving it may be able to see this information.

Where GPS metadata / EXIF location writing is available, it is controlled separately from the visible stamp. Review your settings and media before sharing.

## QR codes

Location QR codes are generated for location-sharing convenience and may contain a map link containing coordinates. Anyone who can scan such a QR code may be able to view that location.

## User-initiated sharing

When you use Android's share features, selected photos, videos, links, or diagnostic information are sent only to the destination you choose.

Opening location information in another map application may pass the relevant coordinates to that application.

## Custom files and media

If you choose a custom logo or other media through Android's system picker, the selected file is used only for the feature you requested unless otherwise stated in the app.

## Diagnostics

Any diagnostics or troubleshooting information generated by the app remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.

## Data selling

The developer does not sell your personal information.

## Children

GPS Camera Stamp - No Ads is a general-purpose camera utility and is not specifically directed at children.

## Data retention

The developer does not operate a user-account database for this app. Media and local preferences remain on your device until you delete them, uninstall the app, clear app data, or Android removes them according to system behavior.

Third-party providers used by optional online features may retain information according to their own privacy policies.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, permissions, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this location with an updated date.
