# Support — GPS Camera Stamp

For help with **GPS Camera Stamp**, please use the options below.

## Before reporting a problem

Please include, when possible:

- phone brand and model;
- Android version;
- app version;
- a short description of what happened;
- steps to reproduce the issue;
- screenshot or screen recording, if useful.

## Common troubleshooting

- Make sure Camera permission is enabled for photo/video capture.
- Enable Location permission if you want GPS coordinates, address, map, or other location-based stamp information.
- Enable Microphone permission if you want sound in recorded videos.
- Check your internet connection for online address, map, or weather features.
- If a location is inaccurate, try again outdoors with a clearer view of the sky and wait briefly for GPS accuracy to improve.

## Feedback

For testing feedback, feature suggestions, or bug reports, use the feedback method provided in the Google Play testing page or the developer contact shown on the Google Play listing.
